## What Propellect is
An **evidence‑first property‑investment analytics** product for Australian investors. Propellect surfaces
official, open market metrics **with full provenance** and helps an investor decide **where** and **which**
property to consider. It **narrates deterministic analysis and never fabricates** a price, rent, yield or
attribute. A live SA "Find My Investment" beta runs at **app.propellect.com.au** (suburb‑level ranking from
open data, with a transparent evidence view).

## What we're building (V8 — "Bring Your Own Deal")
An invite‑only tool where an investor scores a specific property against their saved "buy box". **Today it uses
facts the customer enters manually**, plus a listing URL kept **for reference only — we do not scrape** the
listing site. We want an **authorised commercial route** to *licensed* listing/property data so we can replace
manual entry with a compliant feed.
