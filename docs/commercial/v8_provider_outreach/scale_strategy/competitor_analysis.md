# V8 — Competitor & alternative analysis (Australian property investors)

**Research date:** 2026‑08‑16 (official product/pricing pages where possible). Australian English. Prices are as
published on the dates shown; **marketing claims are the vendor's, not independently verified**. Where a figure
couldn't be confirmed on an official page it is marked **re‑verify**.

## Per‑alternative read (features relevant to Propellect · strengths · gaps · learn · don't‑copy)
**Real Estate Investar** — *the closest direct competitor.*
- Relevant: **Property Analyser** (10‑yr before/after‑tax cash flow + growth forecast), comparable sales, suburb
  reports, portfolio tracker.
- Strengths: mature (since 2006), comprehensive, buyer‑agent adoption.
- Gaps: **10‑year forecasts presented as predictions** (Propellect's opposite — evidence + labelled estimates,
  no fabrication); pricey ($99.90+/mo, no free trial); aggregates 20+ portals (licensing complexity); heavy/complex.
- **Learn:** depth of the cash‑flow analyser; portfolio tracking as retention. **Don't copy:** forecast‑as‑fact
  framing; multi‑portal aggregation; complexity that deters first‑timers.
- Source: https://info.realestateinvestar.com.au/pricing (2026‑08‑16).

**Cotality RP Data for investors** — *authoritative raw data, not decisions.*
- Relevant: suburb insights, rental yields, sales/rental history, maps.
- Strengths: authority + ~98% coverage. Gaps: **data, not a personalised deal decision**; expensive; restrictive
  reuse terms. **Learn:** authority/coverage as a provenance source (also our provider track). **Don't copy:**
  raw‑data‑dump UX; the price point.
- Source: https://www.cotality.com/au/products/rp-data-for-investors (2026‑08‑16).

**Suburbtrends** — *free, honest trend focus.*
- Strengths: free; credible; explicitly avoids over‑claiming price estimates. Gaps: **suburb‑level, not
  property/deal‑level**; no buy‑box scoring. **Learn:** its honesty about *not* estimating prices is a trust model
  worth echoing. **Don't copy:** n/a.
- Source: https://www.suburbtrends.com/ (re‑verify pricing) via https://www2.microburbs.com.au/post/top-10-property-research-tools-for-2024-in-australia (2026‑08‑16).

**Free portals (realestate.com.au / Domain)** — *the incumbent behaviour.*
- Strengths: free, ubiquitous, the listings themselves. Gaps: **no personalised investment analysis**; no buy‑box
  fit; no cash‑flow view. **Learn:** this + a spreadsheet **is** the behaviour we replace. **Don't copy / must
  respect:** their data is licensed — **no scraping**.

**Buyer's agents** — *the expensive, high‑trust alternative.*
- Strengths: full done‑for‑you, expertise, tax‑deductible. Gaps: **$6k–$33k or 1.5–3%**, not self‑serve, not a
  tool. **Learn:** investors *will* pay a lot for **confidence in a specific deal** — that's the job. **Don't
  copy:** their cost structure — Propellect is a **low‑cost tool that makes an investor a better self‑director**,
  not a service (and a potential *referral partner*, not just a competitor).
- Source: https://whichrealestateagent.com.au/buyers-agent/fees/ (2026‑08‑16).

**Spreadsheets / manual research** — *the true default.*
- Strengths: free, flexible. Gaps: error‑prone, no official evidence, no provenance/labelling, slow. **Learn:**
  this is the **primary behaviour to replace** — a Deal Brief must be faster *and* more trustworthy than a DIY sheet.

**Mortgage‑broker analysis** — Strengths: free, serviceability focus. Gaps: financing lens, not deal‑fit; possible
conflicts. **Learn:** brokers are a **distribution channel**, and serviceability is a complementary input.

**AI property tools (emerging)** — Gaps: **hallucinated numbers / undisclosed provenance** are common. **Learn:**
our differentiator is **provenance + no fabrication + honest "missing"**. **Don't copy:** confident made‑up figures.

## The white space Propellect owns
None of the above does: **"score the *specific* deal I'm considering against *my* buy box, with every number
labelled by source, honest about what's missing — in minutes, at low cost."** Real Estate Investar is nearest but
is forecast‑heavy, pricier and complex; Cotality is raw data; buyer's agents are expensive services; portals +
spreadsheets are the default we replace. **Propellect = the honest, deal‑specific decision layer that sits between
"browsing a portal" and "hiring a buyer's agent."**
