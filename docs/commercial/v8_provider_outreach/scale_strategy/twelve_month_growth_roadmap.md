# V8 — Path from 25 to 1,000 customers (stage‑gated, ~12 months)

Australian English. A **recommendation**. Each stage has hard **entry/exit gates** — don't advance on hope, and
**don't build the next stage's machinery early**. Principles preserved throughout: BYOD · manual entry (until a
feed is justified) · no scraping · confirm‑before‑incomplete · labelled evidence · no valuation/advice · no
licensed‑live‑data claim.

## Stage 1 — Founding beta (0 → 25) · ~months 0–1
- **Entry gate:** V8 Preview live + invite‑only gating on + UAT passed (Codex‑owned); kit ready; ≥30 shortlisted.
- **Focus:** direct founder recruitment; product **usefulness**; **trust**; behavioural validation; WTP interviews.
- **Product evidence:** activation ≥60%, usefulness ≥4.0/5, confirm‑before‑incomplete working, labels understood.
- **Customer evidence:** ≥40% 7‑day return; real deals brought; qualitative "would use again".
- **Commercial evidence:** ≥40% WTP signal; a fair‑price band (hypothesis).
- **Data/provider:** **none** — manual entry + open data only.
- **Exit gate:** hit activation/return/usefulness/WTP targets → Stage 2. (See `../../../launch/v8_founding_beta/command_centre/go_no_go_scorecard.md`.)
- **Do NOT build yet:** billing, paid ads, licensed feeds, referral automation, multi‑state.

## Stage 2 — Evidence (25 → 100) · ~months 1–3
- **Entry gate:** Stage 1 exit met; ≥3 strong testimonials **with explicit permission**.
- **Focus:** referrals; testimonials; repeat usage; **first pricing experiment**; 1–2 small partnerships (a broker
  or buyer's agent who refers clients).
- **Product evidence:** repeat‑usage cohort; a working (manual) referral loop.
- **Customer evidence:** referral rate > 0.2; retention holding; NPS‑style advocacy positive.
- **Commercial evidence:** first paid conversions at the tested price; validated (not final) price band.
- **Data/provider:** still manual‑entry primary; **begin provider conversations** (Domain first) — unsent drafts
  become sent only on Abdul's approval.
- **Exit gate:** repeatable interest + early paying users + a channel that works twice → Stage 3.
- **Do NOT build yet:** scaled paid acquisition; a feed you can't yet afford.

## Stage 3 — Repeatability (100 → 300) · ~months 3–7
- **Entry gate:** a **repeatable acquisition channel** identified; pricing validated enough to charge.
- **Focus:** one repeatable channel; **partner distribution**; onboarding polish; retention validation;
  **provider‑agreement decision** (sign a feed only if unit economics justify it — see business model §D).
- **Product evidence:** onboarding activation stable at scale; retention curve flattening.
- **Customer evidence:** CAC:LTV healthy (≥3×); partner‑sourced users converting.
- **Commercial evidence:** predictable MRR growth; provider cost modelled against margin **before** signing.
- **Data/provider:** decide manual‑entry vs licensed feed based on **[PROVIDER_COST]** vs margin; if signing,
  start SA‑only under attribution.
- **Exit gate:** proven channel + acceptable economics (incl. any provider cost) → Stage 4.
- **Do NOT build yet:** national expansion; heavy compliance tooling beyond what a paid launch needs.

## Stage 4 — Scale (300 → 1,000) · ~months 7–12
- **Entry gate:** proven commercial model + healthy economics with provider cost included.
- **Focus:** **controlled paid acquisition**; operational support (docs + tooling to keep support cost/user down);
  data & provider strategy (feed live where it pays); compliance & security maturity (privacy terms, legal review).
- **Product evidence:** stable activation/retention at 1,000; support cost/user trending down.
- **Customer evidence:** paid acquisition with CAC payback < a few months; referral engine contributing.
- **Commercial evidence:** unit economics positive **after** provider/data + support + infra.
- **Data/provider:** licensed feed operating (if signed) SA→multi‑state under the state‑expansion cost gate.
- **Exit gate:** 1,000 customers on a repeatable, margin‑positive model → scale beyond / raise / expand states.
- **Do NOT build:** anything that scrapes, fabricates, or over‑promises; premature multi‑state before each state's
  data gate + cost gate pass.

## Cross‑stage guardrails
- **Never** let provider licensing **block** a manual‑entry beta — the product ships on user facts + open data.
- **Never** present the tool as a valuation or advice, or claim licensed live data before a signed agreement.
- Advance only when the **exit gate evidence** exists; otherwise iterate or (per `../../../launch/v8_founding_beta/command_centre/post_beta_decision_framework.md`)
  extend/narrow/pause.

## The six inputs gate the *commercial* moves (not the beta)
Legal entity/ABN/contact/pilot size/volume/model (see `abdul_decision_recommendations.md`) are needed for
**provider outreach and any paid launch**, not to run the manual‑entry founding beta.
