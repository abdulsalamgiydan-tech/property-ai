# V8 — Market positioning (recommendation)

Australian English. This is a **recommendation** for the founding beta, not a fixed fact. It keeps every product
principle: Bring Your Own Deal · manual fact entry (this beta) · no scraping · confirm before scoring incomplete
info · labelled fact/source/calculation/estimate · **no licensed‑live‑data claim · no valuation / no financial
advice**.

## Recommended narrow initial segment (pick ONE — don't serve everyone)
**Active South Australian residential investors evaluating their *next* purchase** — they already own **1–3
properties**, are **DIY researchers** (portals + spreadsheets), are **hunting now**, and want **confidence on a
specific deal** but won't yet pay a buyer's agent.
- **Why this one:** they have a real "which deal?" job *this month*, enough experience to value evidence (low
  education burden), a clear buy box, and they're reachable via Abdul's network + SA investor communities.
- **Deliberately NOT (yet):** absolute first‑timers (need education + more hand‑holding → later), large‑portfolio
  pros (need depth/integrations → later), interstate investors (SA evidence focus first).

## Secondary future customer
Buyer's agents / mortgage brokers who want a fast, client‑facing **deal‑screening** tool (channel + B2B2C), and
first‑time investors once trust + guidance mature.

## Jobs‑to‑be‑done
> "When I find a property I'm considering, help me **quickly and honestly** judge whether it fits my strategy and
> numbers, so I can shortlist or reject it with confidence — and ask better questions before I commit."

## Most urgent pain
Analysis paralysis + fear of an expensive mistake. Portals show listings but don't judge **fit**; DIY spreadsheets
are error‑prone and lack official evidence; buyer's agents cost **$6k–$33k**; data subs cost **$2k+/yr** and hand
you raw numbers, not a decision.

## Existing behaviour being replaced
Browse realestate.com.au/Domain → copy details into a spreadsheet → guess yield/growth/affordability → hope.

## Category
**Deal‑screening / decision‑support for property investors.** Not a data subscription, not a valuation service,
not a buyer's agent, not a "hotspotting" predictor.

## One‑sentence value proposition
**Propellect turns a property you've found into an honest, evidence‑backed Deal Brief — scored against your own
buy box, in minutes — without the guesswork of a spreadsheet or the bill of a buyer's agent.**

## Three defensible differentiators
1. **Deal‑specific + personalised to your buy box** — not suburb‑level trends, not raw data: it judges *this*
   property against *your* strategy and budget.
2. **Provenance + radical honesty** — every figure is labelled *your fact / official evidence / estimate /
   assumption / missing*; it **won't fabricate** a number and it **shows what's missing** (the opposite of AI
   guesswork and 10‑year "forecast" tools).
3. **Low‑cost, self‑serve, minutes not meetings** — accessible to a DIY investor, versus $99+/mo platforms,
   $2k+/yr data subs, or $6k+ buyer's agents.

## Reasons to believe
Deterministic scoring engine (not a black‑box that hallucinates); per‑field provenance; open official market data;
a transparent "why it may not fit" + "missing evidence" section; confirm‑before‑scoring‑incomplete.

## Likely objections → response
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is this a valuation?" | No — it's decision‑support to think with, clearly not a valuation or advice; estimates are labelled and modelled, do your own due diligence. |
| "Manual entry is annoying." | It's a couple of minutes, and confirming missing facts keeps it honest; a licensed feed is on our roadmap (not claimed as live). |
| "Only SA?" | Yes, on purpose — we start where the official evidence is strong and honest, and expand as coverage passes our data gates. |
| "Why trust the numbers?" | Every number shows its source; we mark "missing" instead of inventing — the opposite of tools that guess. |
| "I can do this in a spreadsheet." | You can — but slower, without official evidence, and with more errors; we do the honest version in minutes. |
| "Is my data safe / private?" | Your buy box + deals are yours, not shared with others; you can delete anytime (formal privacy terms pending legal review). |

## What Propellect should deliberately avoid becoming
A raw‑data reseller · a valuation/AVM tool · a get‑rich "hotspotting" predictor · a buyer's agent (better as a
*partner*) · a bloated all‑in‑one platform · anything that **fabricates numbers or scrapes portals**.

## Messaging hierarchy
- **Landing page:** (1) "Score the deal you're actually considering" (deal‑specific) → (2) "Honest, evidence‑backed,
  every number labelled" (trust) → (3) "In minutes, invite‑only SA beta, free" (access).
- **Onboarding:** buy box → first Deal Brief → understand the labels → bring a real deal.
- **Provider conversations:** strict evidence separation → listing/agent/price kept **free + attributed** →
  Propellect charges only for its **derived** analysis → no scraping, provenance + purge.

## Guardrails (non‑negotiable in all copy)
No valuation/advice claim; no licensed‑live‑data claim; no scraping; honest labelling and "missing"; pricing framed
as hypotheses to validate; legal entity/ABN/contact remain **placeholders** until Abdul supplies them.
