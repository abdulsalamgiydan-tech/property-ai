## Waitlist conversion (fastest quality)
- Segment existing interest by SA + "actively looking"; send the personal invite (`../../../launch/v8_founding_beta/investor_invitation_email.md`).
- Only contact people who opted in; honour unsubscribe. Target: convert enough to fill ~25 with a waitlist buffer.

## Partner motion (Stage 2 seed, start now softly)
- Brokers/accountants/buyer's agents have trust + a stream of investor clients. Offer a **free client‑screening
  tool** angle (a fast, honest Deal Brief they can hand a client). No revenue share promised in beta; explore in
  Stage 2. Keep it a **partnership**, not a data deal.

## Weekly founder routine (~3–5 hrs)
| Day | ~time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 30 min | Review KPI board (`../../../launch/v8_founding_beta/command_centre/kpi_dashboard_template.csv`); pick this week's focus |

| Ongoing | 30–60 min | Respond to participants within 1 business day (`support_playbook`) |

## Rules of the road
- Personal, honest, invite‑only; never claim valuations/advice or licensed data.
- Respect community self‑promotion rules and **Spam Act** consent/unsubscribe expectations.
- Log every prospect in the pipeline template (initials only — no personal data committed to the repo).
- Quality over volume: 25 *engaged* SA investors beat 100 tyre‑kickers.
