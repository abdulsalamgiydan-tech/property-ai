## What Propellect is
An **evidence-first property-investment platform** for Australian investors. Propellect surfaces official,
open-data market metrics with **full provenance** and helps an investor decide **where** and **which**
property to consider — always narrating deterministic analysis, **never fabricating** a price, rent, yield
or attribute.

- **Live today:** an SA "Find My Investment" beta at **app.propellect.com.au** (suburb-level ranking from
  official CC-BY data, with a transparent evidence drawer).

## Ambition
SA first, then **state-by-state nationwide** — but only when a state's data (price, rent, yield, volume,
growth) is present, fresh and licence-cleared. **No nationwide claim until every offered state passes.**

## What we want from a listing-data provider
Authorised access to **on-market SA sale listings** (national later) to power **Deal Hunter**: match live
listings to an investor's saved "buy box", rank them transparently, and produce a one-page **Deal Brief** —
built from **your listing facts + public official market evidence + the user's own financial inputs**.

## The deal we're proposing (respecting your terms)
- **Free to the user, always:** the listing display, **agent contact** (never behind a paywall/login), and
  any provider price/estimate — shown **with your attribution and a link back to the original listing**.
- **What we'd charge for:** *our own* derived analysis — a personalised deal-fit score, conservative
  cash-flow scenarios, alerts, and the Deal Brief. This is Propellect IP layered on top, not your data.
- **We do not scrape** any portal and **do not redistribute raw data** onward.

## Trust & security controls

database access (SECURITY DEFINER consumer RPCs, no client secrets); configurable **caching, retention and
purge** to honour your terms (including delete-on-termination and provider-directed takedown).
