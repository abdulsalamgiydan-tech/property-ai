# Onboarding guide — 5‑minute quick‑start

> Sent when a participant accepts. Gets them to their first Deal Brief fast. Australian English. No
> valuation/advice implication; manual‑entry framing.

**Welcome to the Propellect founding beta.** Here's how to get your first Deal Brief in about five minutes.

## Step 1 — Set your buy box (2 min)
Open the app and set your **strategy** (growth / balanced / cash‑flow), **max price**, **deposit**, and the
**weekly holding cost** you're comfortable with. This is what every property gets scored against. You can change
it any time.

## Step 2 — Bring a deal (2 min)
Go to **Bring Your Own Deal**. Paste the **listing link for reference** (we don't read the page for you), then
type in the facts: **address, suburb, price, property type, beds, baths, parking, status.**
- If you leave something blank, we'll **ask you to confirm** before scoring — we never guess a missing fact.

## Step 3 — Read your Deal Brief (1 min)
You'll get: **fit vs your buy box**, **why it may not fit**, **missing evidence**, **assumptions**, and an
**estimated cash‑flow view**. Look for the labels on every number:
- **Your fact** (what you entered) · **Official evidence** (open market data) · **Propellect estimate** ·
  **Assumption** · **Missing**.

Then **Save**, **Pass**, or **Compare** up to three properties side by side.

## What to do over the month
- Bring **2–3 properties you're genuinely considering** (that's when the tool is most useful).
- Come back when you're weighing a new one.
- Tell us what's confusing, wrong, or missing — the "why it may not fit" and "missing evidence" sections are
  where your feedback helps most.

## A couple of honest reminders
- This is **illustrative analysis to think with — not a valuation, and not financial, tax or legal advice.**
- It's **SA‑focused** in the beta; some suburbs have more official evidence than others (we show you when
  evidence is thin rather than inventing a number).
