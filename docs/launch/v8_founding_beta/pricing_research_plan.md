# Pricing research plan — founding beta

> **No prices are decided here.** This plans experiments + questions to *discover* willingness to pay. Every
> figure below is a **hypothesis requiring validation**, not a recommendation to charge that amount. Australian
> English. Not financial advice.

## Objective
Learn whether SA investors will pay for Propellect's **derived** analysis (Deal Brief / scoring / scenarios) and
in what **shape** and rough **range** — enough to decide post‑beta pricing with evidence, not guesswork.

## What we will NOT do
- Not set or announce a final price during the beta.
- Not anchor participants with a number before they give their own.
- Not imply the analysis is a valuation or advice.

## Candidate structures (hypotheses to test — not chosen)
| Structure | Hypothesis | How we'd test |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly subscription** | Active investors pay a recurring fee for unlimited Deal Briefs + saved buy box | day‑30 Q5/Q6 + interview Q10‑11; test 2–3 price points verbally |
| **Per‑report** | Occasional buyers prefer paying per Deal Brief | offer as an option in Q6; probe in interviews |
| **Beta‑to‑paid conversion** | Founding members convert at a founder rate | measure day‑30 "yes/yes‑at‑price" + an intent‑to‑continue question |
| **Freemium** | Free basic brief, paid depth (scenarios/alerts/compare) | probe which parts feel "worth paying for" |

## Candidate price points (illustrative HYPOTHESES — validate; do not quote to participants as our price)
- Subscription: test reactions around **$X/mo**, **$Y/mo**, **$Z/mo** (fill from interview signals; e.g. a
  common consumer‑tool band). Treat each as a probe, not an offer.
- Per‑report: test around **$P/report**.
> Replace X/Y/Z/P only with values informed by participant answers — never assert them as Propellect's price.

## Methods
1. **Open WTP first** (interview Q10): let them name a fair shape + number unprompted.
2. **Van Westendorp‑lite** (four framing questions): at what price is it *too cheap to trust / a bargain / getting
   expensive / too expensive*? — reveals an acceptable band without us anchoring.
3. **Day‑30 quant** (Q5/Q6): % willing + preferred structure + a fair‑price number (hypothesis field).
4. **Feature‑value probe** (Q7): what must it do before they'd happily pay → ties price to roadmap.

## Analysis & output
- % WTP (target ≥40%), preferred structure distribution, acceptable price band (labelled hypothesis), and the
  top "must‑have before paying" features.
- Feeds the **go/no‑go memo** and a *proposed* (not final) pricing experiment for the next cohort.

## Guardrails
Pricing is a **proposal to validate**, never presented as set. No claim that licensed live listing data underpins
the paid value — the paid value is Propellect's **own** derived analysis on the customer's facts + open data.
