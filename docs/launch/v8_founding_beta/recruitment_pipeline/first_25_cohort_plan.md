# First 25 — controlled 3-wave cohort plan

Australian English. A **staged, evidence-gated** recruitment plan for the first 25 SA founding-beta investors.
The waves are deliberately small so the main journey (buy box → bring your own deal → Deal Brief) is proven
**safe and useful** before more people are exposed to it. **No wave opens until the prior wave's gate passes.**

## Why staged (not all 25 at once)
- The beta is manual-entry and honesty-critical; a mislabelled figure or a confusing journey is cheaper to catch
  with 5 users than 25.
- Small waves let Abdul support each participant personally (~3–5 hrs/week, per `founder_sales_playbook.md`).
- It ties recruitment to **real** activation evidence, not optimism — matching the beta's success targets
  (`../beta_overview.md`).

## The three waves
| Wave | Size | Who | Primary source (see `ranked_shortlist.md`) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wave 1 — Lighthouse** | **5** | Abdul's most-trusted, most-engaged SA investors who will tolerate rough edges and give candid feedback | Abdul's trusted network (1:1, consented) | Prove the journey works safely + usefully; find blocking bugs/confusion |
| **Wave 2 — Validation** | **10** | Warm referrals from Wave 1 + qualifying SA waitlist opt-ins | Referrals + waitlist conversion | Confirm activation + usefulness hold beyond the inner circle |
| **Wave 3 — Consolidation** | **10** | Broader warm sources: partner-referred investors, permission-based community intros | Partner seed + permissioned community | Fill to 25; test whether quality holds via less-warm channels |

**Total: 25 invited.** Maintain a small **waitlist buffer** (3–5) so a decline/no-show doesn't stall a wave.

## Evidence gates (a later wave only opens if the prior wave clears its gate)

### Gate 1 → open Wave 2 (needs Wave-1 evidence)
Open Wave 2 only when, for the 5 Wave-1 users:
- **Safety:** ≥ 1 participant completed the **full journey** (buy box → bring a real deal → Deal Brief) with **no
  mislabelled figures** and **no valuation/advice implication** observed; the "confirm before scoring incomplete
  facts" step worked; no data-isolation or trust incident.
- **Usefulness:** ≥ 3 of 5 activated (completed a buy box) and ≥ 2 rated the Deal Brief **useful (≥ 4/5)** or said
  so in a call.
- **Support load:** issues are triaged and either fixed or have a known workaround; no unresolved blocker.
- **Codex technical GO** for the journey remains valid (this doc does not grant it).

### Gate 2 → open Wave 3 (needs Wave-2 evidence)
Open Wave 3 only when, across the ~15 users then invited:
- Activation (invited → completed buy box) is tracking toward the **≥ 60%** target.
- 7-day return is tracking toward **≥ 40%**; mean usefulness **≥ 4.0/5** holds.
- No trust/safety or labelling regression as volume grew; support stays within Abdul's weekly capacity.

### Gate 3 → willingness-to-pay + go/no-go (needs full-cohort evidence)
With ~25 users: run the WTP/pricing conversation (`../pricing_research_plan.md`), then write the go/no-go memo
(`../command_centre/go_no_go_scorecard.md`). **≥ 40% "yes, at a price"** is the headline WTP signal.

## Screening (applies to every wave)
Use `../screening_form.md`. Prioritise SA investors who are **actively evaluating a deal this quarter**, have
enough experience to value evidence, and will give honest feedback. Quality over volume — **25 engaged SA
investors beat 100 tyre-kickers.**

## Guardrails
Invite-only; consent-based; Spam Act-compliant (honour unsubscribe). No scraping, no bought leads, no inferred
emails. Log prospects with **initials only** in `../command_centre/participant_pipeline_template.csv` — **no
personal data committed to the repo.** Honest positioning per `README.md` at all times.
