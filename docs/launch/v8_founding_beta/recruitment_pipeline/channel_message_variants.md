# Channel message variants (UNSENT)

Australian English. Nine reusable message drafts for Abdul. **All are UNSENT and must stay unsent until Abdul
approves** (`final_launch_approval_packet.md`). Every variant keeps the honest positioning: **manual-entry (you
type the facts), no scraping, no live licensed data, an illustrative Deal Brief — not a valuation and not
financial/tax/legal advice**, invite-only and consent-based. Replace `[SQUARE_BRACKETS]` before use. Keep it
personal; never bulk-send; honour any opt-out.

Complements the longer templates already in `../investor_invitation_email.md` and `../investor_invitation_dm.md`.

## 1. Warm introduction (Abdul's network / referred investor)
> Hi [NAME], you're one of a handful of SA investors I trust to be honest with me. I'm running a small, invite-only
> 30-day beta of **Propellect** — you enter a property's facts by hand, pick the suburb, and it returns a one-page
> **Deal Brief** scored against your own "buy box", with every number labelled by source. To be upfront: it's
> **manual-entry (no scraping), no live licensed data, and it's not a valuation or financial advice** — it's an
> honest analysis on your facts plus open market evidence. I'd value your candid take. Keen to be one of the first
> five? No pressure, and easy to opt out any time.

## Rules for every variant
- Personal and 1:1; **never** a bulk blast or cold list.
- State the honesty caveats (manual-entry, no scraping, no live licensed data, not a valuation/advice) plainly.
- Consent-based only; honour opt-out immediately; log with **initials only**.
- No pressure, no scarcity tricks, no overclaiming. Quality over volume.
