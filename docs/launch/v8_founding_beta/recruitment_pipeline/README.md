# V8 Founding Beta — Recruitment & Partner Pipeline

Australian English. **Documentation only.** This folder is an evidence-backed plan for Abdul to recruit
Propellect's first **25 South Australian founding-beta investors** and to seed a partner pipeline. It touches **no**
application code, Supabase, Vercel, UAT, migrations, Production, or Codex's worktree. Nothing here has been sent,
posted, or actioned — every channel and partner is a **candidate** awaiting Abdul's decision.

## What was and wasn't done
- **Done:** desk research of public/official sources (Aug 2026); a controlled 3-wave cohort plan; a channel scoring
  method + ranked shortlist; a founder recruitment sequence; unsent message variants; a corrected usage/volume
  model; a launch-approval packet.
- **NOT done (hard guardrails honoured):** no one contacted; no groups joined or posted in; no private communities
  scraped; no private personal data collected; no email addresses inferred; no leads bought; no code/infra touched;
  no push/merge/deploy/PR.

## Positioning that every artifact must preserve (honesty guardrails)
Propellect in the beta is **manual-entry** (the participant types the facts; **no scraping**), runs on the
participant's **own facts + open, labelled market evidence** with **no live licensed provider data**, produces an
**illustrative Deal Brief — not a property valuation and not financial, tax or legal advice**, and labels every
figure by source. All recruitment must be **invite-only, consent-based, and Spam Act-compliant** (no unsolicited
bulk contact; honour unsubscribe). See `../beta_overview.md`, `../trust_safety_disclosures.md`, and
`../../../commercial/v8_provider_outreach/scale_strategy/founder_sales_playbook.md`.
