# V8 — Founder decision memo (important decisions only)

Australian English. The few decisions that matter now, each with a **strong recommendation**, the **evidence** and
**trade‑offs**, and a clear line between **recommended default** and **confirmed fact**. Recommendations are
proposals; **facts requiring Abdul are flagged**.

## D1 — Who is the beta *for*? (segment)
- **Recommendation:** narrow to **active SA investors evaluating their next purchase (own 1–3 properties,
  DIY researchers)**. *(recommended default)*
- **Evidence:** they have a live "which deal?" job, value evidence, have a buy box, and are reachable via Abdul's
  network (`../../../commercial/v8_provider_outreach/scale_strategy/market_positioning.md`, `../../../commercial/v8_provider_outreach/scale_strategy/competitor_analysis.md`).
- **Trade‑off:** excludes first‑timers + pros for now (bigger TAM later, but slower trust/education now).

## D2 — What do we charge (and when)?
- **Recommendation:** **don't charge during the beta**; then test **freemium → Pro ≈ A$19/mo** + a per‑report
  ($15) probe. *(recommended default / hypothesis)*
- **Evidence:** positions well below RE Investar ($99.90+/mo) and data subs ($2k+/yr); unit economics healthy at
  Base (`../../../commercial/v8_provider_outreach/scale_strategy/business_model_and_unit_economics.md`).
- **Trade‑off:** freemium adds free‑user support load; a licensed feed later could compress margin (model it first).
- **Fact required:** Abdul confirms the pricing‑experiment framing (no public price announced).

## D3 — Provider data now or later?
- **Recommendation:** **run the beta manual‑entry (no provider dependency)**; begin **Domain** conversations in
  Stage 2; sign a feed only when economics justify `[PROVIDER_COST]` (Stage 3). *(recommended default)*
- **Evidence:** provider pricing is unpublished and could halve margin; manual entry validates value with zero
  data cost (`../../../commercial/v8_provider_outreach/scale_strategy/provider_negotiation_strategy.md`, business model §D).
- **Trade‑off:** manual entry is more effort for users (mitigated by confirm‑before‑incomplete + minimal fields).
- **Fact required:** Abdul's six inputs + approval before any outreach.

## D4 — Pilot size
- **Recommendation:** **cap at 25** SA investors. *(recommended default)*
- **Evidence:** enough signal for the four targets; small enough for founder‑led support in 3–5 hrs/week.
- **Trade‑off:** limits raw volume of feedback; raise only if demand clearly exceeds supply.

## D5 — Go/No‑Go trigger
- **Recommendation:** launch only on a **GO** or **CONDITIONAL GO** from `go_no_go_scorecard.md` (esp. technical
  readiness + trust/safety). *(recommended default)*
- **Trade‑off:** none — launching before the product + disclaimers are ready risks trust.
- **Fact required:** technical GO/NO‑GO is **Codex‑owned**.

## Confirmed facts vs recommendations
- **Confirmed:** product principles (no scraping, labelling, confirm‑incomplete, not‑advice/valuation); the four
  success targets; provider prices are unpublished; the researched competitor prices (as at 2026‑08‑16).
- **Recommendations (not yet decided):** segment, pricing experiment, provider timing, pilot cap.
