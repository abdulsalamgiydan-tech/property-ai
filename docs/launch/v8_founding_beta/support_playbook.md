# Support playbook — founding beta

> Internal. How we respond to participants during the 30 days. Keep it fast and human. Australian English.

## Channels & response targets
- **Primary:** reply to the invite/onboarding thread (email or DM).
- **Target response:** within **1 business day**; same‑day for anyone blocked from getting a first Deal Brief.
- Founder‑led during the beta (25 people is small enough to be personal).

## Triage
1. **Blocked from activating** (can't set buy box / can't get a first brief) → **highest priority**; unblock same
   day, then log the friction.
2. **Confused by output** (labels, "why it may not fit", missing evidence) → explain + capture as a
   product‑clarity note (see `feedback_taxonomy.md`).
3. **Bug / wrong result** → reproduce, log with the property inputs, tag severity; never dismiss a "this looks
   wrong".
4. **Feature request** → acknowledge, log, don't over‑promise.

## Canned responses (edit to sound human)
- **Missing‑fact confusion:** "Leave it blank — we'll ask you to confirm, and the brief marks it as *missing*
  rather than guessing. That 'missing' flag is useful signal, not a failure."
- **"Is this a valuation?":** "No — it's illustrative analysis to think with, not a valuation or financial advice.
  The estimate is modelled from your inputs plus open data. Do your own due diligence."
- **Thin coverage in a suburb:** "Some SA suburbs have richer official evidence than others. We show 'missing'
  instead of inventing a number — if it's thin there, that's an honest limitation we're working on."
- **Scraping question:** "We don't read the listing site for you — you enter the facts. A licensed data feed is
  something we're exploring separately."

## Escalation
- Anything touching **legal/financial claims, data deletion requests, or a participant upset about results** →
  escalate to Abdul directly; do not improvise policy.
- **Do not** make pricing commitments during support — point to "pricing isn't set yet".

## Logging (every interaction)
Record: participant (initials), date, category (`feedback_taxonomy.md`), the deal inputs if relevant, resolution,
and any product change it implies. Feeds the weekly review + `beta_kpi_tracker_spec.md`.

## Weekly cadence (see beta_overview)
Mon: pull metrics + friction board. Wed: 3 user calls. Fri: ship ≤1 proven fix + send a short "what changed" note.

## Boundaries
Support is help + listening, **not** advice. We never tell a participant whether to buy. We never present output
as a valuation. We never claim licensed live listing data.
