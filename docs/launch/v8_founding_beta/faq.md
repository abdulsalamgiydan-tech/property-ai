# FAQ — SA founding beta

> Plain‑language answers. Australian English. Reinforce: not a valuation/advice; manual entry; no scraping; no
> claim of licensed live listing data.

**Is this financial advice?**
No. Propellect gives **illustrative analysis to think with — it is not a property valuation and not financial,
tax or legal advice.** Always do your own due diligence and seek qualified advice before buying.

**Do I have to enter everything by hand?**
Yes, for now. You paste the listing link **for reference** and type the facts. **We don't scrape** listing sites.
Moving to a licensed data feed is something we're exploring separately — we won't pretend we have it until we do.

**What if I don't know a detail (e.g. land size)?**
Leave it blank. We'll **ask you to confirm** before scoring, and the Deal Brief will clearly mark it as
**missing** rather than guessing.

**Where do the numbers come from?**
Every figure is labelled: **Your fact** (what you entered), **Official evidence** (open market data with its
source), **Propellect estimate** (our model), **Assumption**, or **Missing**. Nothing is invented.

**Is the "estimate" a valuation?**
No. It's a modelled illustration from your inputs plus open data — not a valuation and not a price guarantee.

**Which areas are covered?**
The beta is **South Australia**‑focused. Some suburbs have richer official evidence than others; we show you when
evidence is thin instead of filling gaps with made‑up numbers.

**Is my data private?**
Your buy box and the deals you enter are stored to provide the analysis and improve the product; they're **not**
shared with other participants. You can ask us to delete your beta data anytime.

**How much time will it take?**
About **20 minutes across the month**, plus a short chat and two quick surveys (day 7 and day 30).

**Is it free? Will it cost later?**
Free during the 30‑day beta. We may invite you to a paid plan afterwards — **no obligation**, and pricing will be
transparent (nothing is decided yet).

**Can I compare properties?**
Yes — you can compare up to **three** side by side.

**Who do I contact for help?**
Reply to your invite/onboarding message. See `support_playbook.md` for response times.

**Can I invite a friend?**
Places are limited to 25 SA investors. If you know someone who'd be a great fit, let us know and we'll consider
them for the waitlist.
