# Participant expectations — founding beta

> A short "what to expect" agreement, plain language. Sets a two‑way contract of effort + honesty. Australian
> English. Not a legal document; not advice.

## What you get
- Early access to Propellect's **Bring Your Own Deal**, free for the 30‑day beta.
- A one‑page **Deal Brief** for each property you enter, matched to your buy box.
- Direct line to the founder; your feedback shapes the roadmap.

## What we ask of you
- **Bring 2–3 real properties** you're actually weighing up.
- Spend **~20 minutes across the month**, and do **one short interview** (~20 min).
- Complete two quick surveys (**day 7** and **day 30**).
- Give **honest feedback**, including "this isn't useful yet".

## How it works (and its limits)
- **Manual entry:** you type the facts; the listing link is **reference only**; **we don't scrape** listing sites.
- **We won't guess:** if key facts are missing, you'll confirm before we score.
- **Labelled evidence:** every figure shows its source (your fact / official / estimate / assumption / missing).
- **SA focus:** coverage is best where official evidence exists; we tell you when it's thin.

## Important — what Propellect is not
- **Not a property valuation.** **Not financial, tax or legal advice.** It's illustrative analysis to help you
  think and ask better questions. **Always do your own due diligence** and seek qualified advice before buying.
- We make **no claim** that live listing‑provider data is currently licensed — the beta uses your own facts plus
  open, labelled market evidence.

## Privacy & your data
- We store what you enter (your buy box and the deals you submit) to give you the analysis and improve the
  product. Your individual data isn't shared with other participants.
- You can ask us to delete your beta data at any time.
- Feedback may be used **anonymised and aggregated** to improve Propellect.

## After the beta
We may invite you to continue on a paid plan. There's **no obligation**, and we'll always be upfront about
pricing (see `pricing_research_plan.md` for how we're approaching it — nothing is set yet).
