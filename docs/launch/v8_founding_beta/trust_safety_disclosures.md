# Trust & safety disclosures — founding beta

> **Practical product copy for later legal review.** This is **not legal advice and not final terms** — it's
> plain‑language disclosure copy the product/support can use now and a lawyer can refine. Australian English.

## 1. "Not a valuation / not advice" (show near every Deal Brief + at sign‑up)
> Propellect provides **illustrative analysis to help you think — it is not a property valuation and not
> financial, tax, or legal advice.** Figures are labelled by source; estimates are modelled from your inputs plus
> open data and may be wrong. **Always do your own due diligence and seek qualified professional advice before
> making a purchase decision.**

## 2. Source labelling (why numbers are tagged)
> Every figure shows where it came from: **Your fact** (what you entered) · **Official evidence** (open market
> data, with its source) · **Propellect estimate** (our model) · **Assumption** · **Missing** (we don't have it —
> we won't invent it).

## 3. Manual entry & no scraping
> You enter the property's facts yourself and paste the listing link **for reference only**. **Propellect does not
> scrape, copy, or automatically extract** content from listing websites. You're responsible for the accuracy of
> what you enter.

## 4. No claim of licensed live listing data
> During the beta, Propellect uses **your entered facts** plus **open, labelled market data**. We make **no claim**
> that any third‑party listing‑provider data (e.g. Domain, PropTrack, Cotality) is currently licensed or
> integrated. Any future licensed feed will be introduced only under a signed agreement and disclosed clearly.

## 5. Incomplete submissions
> If key facts are missing, we ask you to **confirm before scoring** and mark those items as **missing** in the
> brief. A score on incomplete facts is lower‑confidence by design.

## 6. Data handling (plain language — for legal review)
> - We store your **buy box** and the **deals you enter** to provide the analysis and improve the product.
> - Your individual data is **not shared with other participants**.
> - You can request **deletion** of your beta data at any time.
> - Feedback may be used **anonymised and aggregated** to improve Propellect.
> *(A formal privacy policy / terms should be prepared and legally reviewed before any paid launch.)*

## 7. Beta status & availability
> This is an **invite‑only beta**; features may change or break, and coverage is **South Australia‑focused** with
> stronger evidence in some suburbs than others. It's **free** during the 30‑day beta; any future paid plan will
> be optional and transparently priced.

## 8. Accuracy & liability (plain language — for legal review)
> We work hard to be honest and provenance‑labelled, but estimates and open‑data figures can be inaccurate or
> out of date. Propellect isn't liable for decisions made using the tool; it's an input to your own research.
> *(Final liability wording must be set by a qualified adviser.)*
