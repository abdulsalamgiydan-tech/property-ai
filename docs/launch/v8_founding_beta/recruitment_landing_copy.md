## How it works (three steps)
1. **Enter the facts.** Paste the listing link for reference and type in the key details — address, price, type,
   beds, baths, parking. (We don't read the listing site for you.)
2. **Match your buy box.** We score it against the strategy and budget you set.
3. **Get your Deal Brief.** Fit, risks, missing evidence, assumptions and an estimated cash‑flow view — clearly
   separating **your facts**, **official market evidence**, and **our estimates**.
