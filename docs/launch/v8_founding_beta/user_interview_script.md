# User interview script — founding beta (~20 min)

> Semi‑structured. Goal: learn whether it's useful, whether they'd pay, and what's missing. Listen more than you
> talk. Don't lead. Australian English. Not advice.

## Before (1 min)
"Thanks for making time. This is about learning what works and what doesn't — please be blunt; 'this isn't useful'
is the most valuable thing you can tell me. No right answers. OK if I take notes?"

## Warm‑up (2 min)
1. Tell me about your investing right now — what are you trying to do this year?
2. When you're weighing up a property today, what do you actually do? What tools/spreadsheets?

## The task (5 min) — watch them use it
3. Bring a property you're genuinely considering and walk me through entering it. *(Observe: where do they
   hesitate? what's confusing? do they trust the labels?)*
4. What did you expect to happen vs what happened?
5. Look at the Deal Brief — what's useful? What's noise? What don't you believe, and why?

## Value & trust (5 min)
6. Did this tell you anything you didn't already know, or change how you'd think about this deal?
7. The numbers are labelled *your fact / official evidence / estimate / assumption / missing* — did that land? Did
   you trust the "estimate"?
8. Where did it feel wrong or thin? What would make you trust it more?

## Willingness to pay (4 min) — don't anchor
9. If this existed properly, would you use it regularly? For what?
10. Would you **pay** for it? *(let them answer before offering numbers)* — what would feel fair, and in what
    shape (subscription, per‑report, other)?
11. What would it need to do before you'd pay? *(This is the roadmap gold.)*

## Close (1 min)
12. Anything I didn't ask that I should have?
"Really helpful — I'll follow up with what we change."

## After (log immediately)
Capture verbatim quotes, top‑3 frictions, usefulness read, WTP read + any price mentioned (as a hypothesis),
tagged per `feedback_taxonomy.md`. Feed `pricing_research_plan.md` and the KPI tracker.
